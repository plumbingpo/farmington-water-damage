Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.
  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.

How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • The breaker box, a running appliance or cords along the floor sit in the path of the water.
